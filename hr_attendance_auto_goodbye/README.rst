===============================
HR Attendance Auto Goodbye
===============================

* Forces the HR Attendance kiosk greetings screen (check-in / check-out
  confirmation) to automatically return to the main kiosk menu after 5
  seconds, simulating a click on the "OK" / "Goodbye" button.

Features
========

* Overrides the kiosk greetings auto-return timer with a fixed 5 second delay,
  regardless of the ``attendance_kiosk_delay`` configured on the company.
* Compatible with Odoo 18 and the standard HR Attendance public kiosk
  workflow (OWL).

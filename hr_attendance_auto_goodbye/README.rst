===============================
HR Attendance Auto Goodbye
===============================

* Automatically closes the "More than 12 hours" warning message in 
  HR Attendance kiosk mode after 5 seconds, simulating the "Goodbye" button click.

Features
========

* Detects when the warning message appears after a long absence.
* Waits 5 seconds and automatically returns to the main kiosk menu.
* Compatible with Odoo 14 and the standard HR Attendance kiosk workflow.
